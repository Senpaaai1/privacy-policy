6. Backup and Data Export

The App includes manual export features that allow you to export your data in several formats. All exports are initiated exclusively by you — they never happen automatically.

The App offers three export options:

  • JSON backup — exports all your data as a single JSON file for backup and restore purposes;
  • PDF report — generates a formatted report of your vehicle's service history as a PDF file;
  • Excel report (.xlsx) — generates a structured spreadsheet with service history, document expiry dates, and cost summaries for all your vehicles.

When you trigger an export, the App creates a file on your device and presents the standard Android sharing sheet. You may then choose to:

  • Save the file to a local folder on your device (via the Storage Access Framework);
  • Share it to Google Drive or any other app available in your sharing sheet.

Important: Once you share the file to a third-party service (e.g., Google Drive), that service's own Privacy Policy governs how the file is stored and processed. The developer has no access to files you choose to store in third-party services.

The App also respects the Android system setting android:allowBackup="false", meaning Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled — your App data is not backed up by Google's automatic backup service without your knowledge.

7. Push Notifications

Notifications in this App are local notifications — they are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server. No third-party push notification service (e.g., Firebase Cloud Messaging) is used for targeted messaging.

You can disable notifications at any time via your device's notification settings.

8. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The developer does not share, sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose your data to any third party. Because no data leaves your device through the App itself, there is no data transmission to third parties on our part.

The App uses the following open-source framework and libraries, none of which independently collect or transmit personal data:

  • React Native / Expo — cross-platform mobile framework
  • AsyncStorage — local, on-device storage
  • expo-file-system — local file operations
  • expo-image-picker — camera and gallery access
  • expo-notifications — local notification scheduling
  • expo-sharing — Android share sheet integration
  • react-native-svg — chart rendering
  • expo-print — local PDF generation
  • expo-document-picker — file selection for backup import (system picker, no data transmitted)
  • expo-haptics — device vibration feedback on user actions
  • xlsx-js-style — local Excel (.xlsx) file generation for data export
  • expo-localization — detects device language to display the correct app language
  • expo-splash-screen — controls the app launch screen
  • expo-store-review — prompts users to rate the app on Google Play (no personal data collected or transmitted)
  • react-native-calendars — calendar UI component for date selection

These libraries operate locally and do not independently collect analytics or personal data.

The developer holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot delete it on your behalf, nor restore it if you uninstall the App without a prior backup.
